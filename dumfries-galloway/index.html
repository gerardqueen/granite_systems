How it works

From enquiry to fitted

    Enquire

    Use the form or call 01592 654386. We'll talk through material, edges and cut-outs and send a price.

Ultra-compact surface by Cosentino

Dekton

Dekton is made by fusing minerals under extreme heat and pressure, much as natural stone forms over thousands of years. It contains no resins, so bleach, oven cleaner and drain cleaner won't mark it, and it's more scratch-resistant than granite, which makes it ideal for outdoor kitchens and busy commercial spaces.

  • Suitable for outdoor kitchens
  • More scratch-resistant than granite
  • Unaffected by household chemicals

Natural stone

Marble

Marble has been prized for millennia, and no two pieces are the same. It's softer and more porous than granite or quartz, so it gains a lived-in patina over time, which is why we often recommend it for bathrooms, fireplaces and feature pieces rather than busy kitchens.

  • Polished or honed finish
  • Ideal for vanities, hearths and feature pieces
  • Every installation is unique

Solid surface

Corian

Corian is a blend of natural minerals and acrylic that can be joined invisibly and heat-formed into curves, so worktops and upstands flow as one surface. It's non-porous and resists most chemicals, and unlike stone, everyday marks can be repaired.

  • Invisible joins and thermoformed curves
  • Non-porous and hygienic
  • Repairable surface
