Privacy notice

We only use your details to answer your enquiry. We don't sell them, share them for marketing, or use tracking cookies.

Who we are

This website is run by Granite Systems Ltd, Unit 6 Randolph Court, Randolph Industrial Estate, Kirkcaldy, Fife KY1 2YY. Granite Systems Ltd is the data controller for the details you give us on this website.

Questions about your data: sales@granitesystems.co.uk or 01592 654386.

What we collect

When you ask for a quote, we keep:

  • your name, email address and phone number (if you give it);
  • your postcode, what the work is for, and which page you sent it from;
  • anything you write in the message box.

Browsing the site doesn't collect anything about you. Nothing is sent until you press Request my quote.

Why we use it

To prepare your quote, arrange templating and fitting, and contact you about that job. The legal basis is that you've asked us to take steps towards a contract with you. We won't add you to a mailing list.

Who handles it for us

We use a small number of service providers to run the site. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase stores your enquiry and the job photos shown on the site (our database).
  • Resend sends us an email when a new request arrives, containing the details you entered.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and, like any web host, briefly logs visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts, so it sees your IP address when the page loads.
  • YouTube plays our video. We use its privacy-enhanced mode, so YouTube doesn't set cookies until you press play.

Some of these providers may store data outside the UK. Where they do, they use the safeguards UK data protection law requires, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

We keep requests for 12 months so we can deal with follow-up questions, then delete them. If you go ahead and buy, we keep the order details for as long as the law requires for our business records (usually 6 years).

Cookies and storage

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. The staff login page stores a sign-in token in the browser, and that's only used by our staff.

Job photos and reviews

We only publish photos of a job and a customer's review with that customer's agreement, and we show reviews by first name and area only. Ask us and we'll take them down.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your details, to correct them, or to delete them, and you can object to how we use them. Email or phone us using the details above. If you're not happy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
