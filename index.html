How it works

Four steps, one team

  1. 1

    Enquire

    Send the form or call. Rough sizes or a kitchen plan are plenty for a quote.

  2. 2

    Digital template

    Once your units are in, we measure on site digitally. No board templates, no mess.

  3. 3

    Made in Kirkcaldy

    Cut on our computerised machinery, then hand finished by skilled stonemasons.

  4. 4

    Fitted

    Our own fitters install it, cut-outs and all, and leave it clean.
